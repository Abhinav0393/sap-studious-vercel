Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2026 · SAP Studios, Lakdikapul, Hyderabad

1. Overview & Static Site Architecture

SAP Studios (founded 2024, Lakdikapul, Hyderabad) operates this static informational website (https://sapstudios.in/) to showcase our architectural and interior design portfolio. Our website does not utilize analytics software, marketing pixels, or third-party tracking beacons. We do not set non-essential cookies on your device.

2. Information You Provide Directly

When you contact us or submit an enquiry through our contact page, the details you type—along with your IP address and browser information—are transmitted by email directly to design@sapstudios.in. Enquiries are used only to reply to you and discuss potential design commissions; they are never stored in any database on the website.

To protect our studio from automated spam, a hashed (non-readable) version of your IP address is kept temporarily on the server for rate limiting, after which it is discarded.

3. Data Confidentiality & Retention

We treat all client designs, drawings, project briefs, and personal communications with strict professional confidentiality. We never sell, rent, or distribute personal information to third parties. Information shared with us is retained only as long as necessary for the design collaboration or as required by applicable laws.

4. External Services & Links

Our website includes links to external services, including Google Maps (for directions to our studio) and Instagram (for project photography). The Contact page displays an interactive Google map; loading this map transmits your IP address and browser details to Google. When you access external links or third-party embeds, their respective privacy policies apply.
